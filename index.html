Sour Grape is a bakery-café and taproom in Oromocto — “Bakery & Taproom” in its own words — pairing homemade baking with locally brewed craft beer on tap and a patio to enjoy it on.

Bakery

Homemade Hand Pies

Homemade hand pies lead the bakery side, alongside a cinnamon biscuit and cheesecake — the sweet-and-savoury case the café is known for.
